# Elgato Marketplace Plugin Refund — to follow up

Researched 2026-08-06. Not yet actioned — this is the process to follow.

## Process (in order)

1. **Contact the plugin's maker first, not Elgato.** Marketplace plugins are
   sold by third-party developers, not Elgato directly. Go to the plugin's
   page on the Marketplace and use its support/contact channel to reach the
   maker.
2. **Give the maker 5 business days to respond.**
3. **Escalate to Elgato Support** — but only after step 2, and only if:
   - the maker hasn't responded within 5 business days, or
   - the maker responded but couldn't resolve a verifiable issue
   Escalate via **"Chat with us"** or **"Contact"** on the Elgato Marketplace
   website. Bring proof of the prior communication with the maker.

## What actually qualifies for a refund
- A verifiable technical issue that breaks the plugin's core advertised
  function
- The plugin being materially/objectively different from what was
  described or previewed on its page

## What works against a refund
- **14-day window** — requests outside that generally aren't considered
- **Already downloaded/installed** — refunds after download are only
  granted in "exceptional circumstances"
